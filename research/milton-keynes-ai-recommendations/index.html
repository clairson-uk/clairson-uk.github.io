Why the answers differ

Each assistant reads different websites.

A strong Google rating helps with Google's AI. The other three also lean on comparison sites, portals, review aggregators, awards and “best of” lists. An agency that looks different on each of them gets mixed results.

ChatGPTMap ratings like Google's, plus local directories such as MKLBD and DiscoverMK.
PerplexityRightmove, GetAgent, Homemove, agencies' own websites, awards (allAgents, Best Estate Agent Guide) and local news (MKFM).

What this means for agents

Being great on Google isn't enough.

  • Make your name, address and phone number match everywhere: Google, GetAgent, Rightmove, Zoopla and your website.
  • Claim and complete your comparison-site profiles, and keep reviews coming on more than one site.
  • Enter awards and make sure accurate “best of” lists include you. Two of the four assistants quoted awards as reasons.
  • Ask the questions yourself every month. The answers change with the question and over time.

Method and limits
  • Three questions, asked word for word: “Who is the best letting agent in Milton Keynes?”, “What are the top-rated estate agents in Milton Keynes?” and “Best property management company in Milton Keynes for landlords?”
  • Four assistants: ChatGPT (signed in, web search on), Google AI Mode (signed in), Copilot Search in Bing (signed out; the Copilot app required a sign-in) and Perplexity (signed out).
  • Perplexity was asked on 30 October 2026 at about 10pm; the others on 31 October 2026 between 3:20pm and 3:40pm.
  • “Named” means recommended or listed as an option. Agencies that appeared only in a fee comparison were not counted.
  • One run per question. AI answers vary by person, time and settings, so this is a snapshot, not a ranking of service quality.
  • No agency paid to be included. None of the agencies named are Clairson clients. For the full answer log, email clairson.uk@gmail.com.
