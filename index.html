    Questions

    Straight answers.

    What is AI visibility?

    It's whether AI assistants like ChatGPT and Google's AI mention your agency when someone asks for a recommendation, and whether what they say about you is accurate.

    What does the free check include?

    We ask the questions landlords and tenants ask on four AI assistants, check your listings on the sites they read, and send you a short report with your top fixes. No cost and no obligation.

    Will you need my passwords?

    No. You add us as a manager on your Google profile and similar accounts. You stay the owner and can remove us at any time.

    Do I have to change my website provider?

    No. We prepare the pages and code, and your current provider adds them. If you'd like, we can talk to them directly.

    How long until I see results?

    Listing fixes usually show within days to weeks. AI answers can take weeks to months to change, which is why we re-test and report every month.

    Do you write reviews?

    Never. We set up a routine so your real customers leave reviews. Fake reviews break the rules of every platform and can get you removed.
