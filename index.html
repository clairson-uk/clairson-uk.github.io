    What we usually find

    Great agencies, invisible for fixable reasons.

    These come up again and again when we check agencies. None of them are about how good you are. All of them are fixable.

    Duplicate listings

    Fixable

    Your reputation split across several pages, some empty, some misspelt.

    Names and addresses that don't match

    Fixable

    Two trading names or three addresses online, so AI can't connect them.

    Unclaimed profiles

    Fixable

    No phone number, no description, and nobody replying to reviews.

    Reviews only on Google

    Fixable

    Comparison sites show a handful of old reviews instead of your real reputation.

    No local pages

    Fixable

    Nothing that ties you to the towns and neighbourhoods you actually cover.

    Questions

    Straight answers.

    What is AI visibility?

    It's whether AI assistants like ChatGPT and Google's AI mention your agency when someone asks for a recommendation, and whether what they say about you is accurate.

    What does the free check include?

    We ask the questions landlords and tenants ask on four AI assistants, check your listings on the sites they read, and send you a short report with your top fixes. No cost and no obligation.

    What's the difference between Standard and Premium?

    Standard fixes how AI sees you: listings, local pages, reviews, a monthly check and your own dashboard. Premium adds an assistant that answers enquiries on your website and WhatsApp when the office is closed, improvements to your current website, and monthly tracking against your top three local rivals.

    What does the enquiry assistant say to people?

    Only what you approve: your fees, areas, opening hours and answers to common questions. For anything else, it takes the person's details and passes them straight to your branch.

    Will you need my passwords?

    No. You add us as a manager on your Google profile and similar accounts. You stay the owner and can remove us at any time.

    Do I have to change my website provider?

    No. We prepare the pages and code, and your current provider adds them. If you'd like, we can talk to them directly.

    How long until I see results?

    Listing fixes usually show within days to weeks. AI answers can take weeks to months to change, which is why we re-test and report every month.

    Do you write reviews?

    Never. We set up a routine so your real customers leave reviews. Fake reviews break the rules of every platform and can get you removed.
