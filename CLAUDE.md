## 2. Key insight (the edge)
AI tools build answers from many sources, not just Google. Directory/comparison sites like **GetAgent** and **Zoopla** matter. Many agencies have great Google reviews but **duplicate, misspelt, unclaimed or review-less listings** elsewhere, or **different business names** across sites. That gap is provable on a phone in 10 seconds and is fixable.

## 5. Audit system (follow exactly)
Platforms: ChatGPT, Google (AI Overview + Gemini), Copilot, Perplexity. Use a private/logged-out window, note date/time, screenshot every answer.

Questions (word for word):
1. Who is the best letting agent in Milton Keynes?
2. What are the top-rated estate agents in Milton Keynes?
3. Which letting agent should I use to rent out my property in Milton Keynes?
4. Best property management company in Milton Keynes for landlords?
5. Letting agents in [agency's area]?
6. I'm looking to rent a flat in Milton Keynes. Which agents should I contact?
7. Tell me about [Agency] in Milton Keynes.
8. Is [Agency] a good letting agent?

Scoring Q1–Q6 per platform: 3 = top 3, 2 = position 4–10, 1 = passing mention, 0 = not mentioned. Max 72. Visibility % = points/72. Bands: 0–20 Invisible, 21–50 Weak, 51–75 Good, 76–100 Strong. Q7–Q8: note factual errors.

Health check: Google profile complete; recent reviews + replies; same name/address/phone across Google, GetAgent, Zoopla, Rightmove, Yell, website; no duplicate/misspelt listings; directory ratings match Google; profiles claimed; website area pages/FAQs/About; local mentions.

Report sections: score + rating (or "pending AI test"), what we found (exact figures + source + date), who AI recommends instead, why it matters (2 sentences), top 3 specific fixes, next step (fixes + monthly re-test), honest promise.

Directory sources used: https://www.getagent.co.uk/area/milton-keynes/estate-agents (+ ?page=2, ?page=3), /area/bletchley/estate-agents, /postcode/<pc>/estate-agents; https://www.zoopla.co.uk/find-agents/letting-agents/milton-keynes/ (+ ?pn=2), /find-agents/estate-agents/mk2/.
